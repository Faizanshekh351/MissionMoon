## 🌌 Overview

**Mission Moon** is an interactive, high-fidelity 3D aerospace traversal vertical slice. The player takes the role of **Pilot Zaddr**, a cybernetically enhanced operative equipped with dual supersonic ion thrusters and zero-G stabilizers. The mission directive is to ascend 18 celestial monolithic hurdles spanning from Earth's lush grassland meadow through the stratosphere, exosphere, and orbital space, ultimately reaching the summit of Planet Earth to board the Apollo-class Lunar Starship for escape velocity.

The game is distributed as:
1. **Standalone 3D WebGL Single-File Engine** (`index.html` / `MissionMoon.html`) featuring real-time WebGL rendering, custom procedural shaders, Web Audio acoustic synthesis, and responsive mobile/desktop inputs.
2. **Unreal Engine 5.4 High-End C++ Architecture** with full support for Lumen hardware ray tracing, Nanite sub-pixel geometry, Niagara vector fields, and arbitrary gravity alignment.

## 📐 Mathematical Formulation & Game Physics

Mission Moon's flight mechanics, orbital trajectories, and collision systems were derived mathematically and implemented with deterministic numerical methods.

### 1. Low-Gravity Kinematic Motion & Gravity Attenuation
The net acceleration acting on Pilot Zaddr at vertical position $y$ and velocity $\vec{v}$ is formulated as:

$$\vec{a}(t) = \vec{g}(y) + \frac{\vec{F}_{\text{thruster}}(t)}{m} - \frac{1}{2m} \rho(y) C_d A \|\vec{v}\| \vec{v}$$

Where:
- $\vec{g}(y)$ represents gravitational acceleration attenuated across atmospheric sectors:
  $$g(y) = g_0 \cdot \left( \frac{R_{\text{Earth}}}{R_{\text{Earth}} + y} \right)^2$$
- In the WebGL implementation, discrete semi-implicit Euler integration preserves kinetic stability at 60 FPS:
  $$\vec{v}_{t+\Delta t} = \vec{v}_t + \vec{a}_t \Delta t$$
  $$\vec{p}_{t+\Delta t} = \vec{p}_t + \vec{v}_{t+\Delta t} \Delta t$$

### 2. Multi-Tier Collision Resolution & Surface Snap
Obstacle interaction is split into swept vertical landing detection and horizontal Separating Axis Theorem (SAT) projection:

1. **Swept Vertical Landing Condition:**
   $$y_{\text{prev}} \ge y_{\text{platform}}^{\max} - \epsilon \quad \land \quad y_{\text{curr}} \le y_{\text{platform}}^{\max} + \delta \quad \land \quad (x, z) \in \Omega_{\text{platform}}$$
   When satisfied:
   $$y_{\text{curr}} \leftarrow y_{\text{platform}}^{\max} + y_{\text{offset}}, \quad v_y \leftarrow 0, \quad \text{isGrounded} \leftarrow \text{true}$$

2. **Lateral Box Ejection (Minimal Penetration Depth):**
   Given character bounding cylinder with radius $r_{\text{char}}$ and platform box $[x_{\min}, x_{\max}] \times [z_{\min}, z_{\max}]$:
   $$d_{\text{left}} = x - (x_{\min} - r_{\text{char}}), \quad d_{\text{right}} = (x_{\max} + r_{\text{char}}) - x$$
   $$d_{\text{back}} = z - (z_{\min} - r_{\text{char}}), \quad d_{\text{front}} = (z_{\max} + r_{\text{char}}) - z$$
   $$\Delta \vec{p} = \arg\min_{d \in \{d_{\text{left}}, d_{\text{right}}, d_{\text{back}}, d_{\text{front}}\}} (d)$$

### 3. Harmonic Bridge Oscillation
Moving kinetic sky-bridges track simple harmonic motion with frequency $\omega$ and amplitude $A$:

$$x(t) = x_{\text{base}} + A \cdot \sin(\omega t + \phi)$$
$$v_x(t) = A \omega \cdot \cos(\omega t + \phi)$$

Frictional inertial transfer imparts $\Delta v_x$ directly to the player when standing on an oscillating surface:
$$\vec{v}_{\text{player}} \mathrel{+}= \vec{v}_{\text{platform}} \cdot \Delta t$$

### 4. Supersonic Plasma Thruster Waveform
The 5-layer exhaust flame simulates Mach shock diamonds through standing acoustic wave interference:

$$\Psi_{\text{flame}}(t, y) = \sum_{k=1}^{3} A_k \sin\left(\omega_k t - \kappa_k y\right) \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{y}{\lambda}\right)$$

Where:
- $\omega_1 = 48 \, \text{rad/s}$ (high-frequency turbulent micro-flicker of white-hot core)
- $\omega_2 = 28 \, \text{rad/s}$ (intermediate combustion pulse of orange mantle)
- $\omega_3 = 14 \, \text{rad/s}$ (aerodynamic plume breathing of cyan outer envelope)
- Periodic shock diamond nodes occur at spatial intervals $y_n = n \cdot d_{\text{nozzle}} \sqrt{M^2 - 1}$.
