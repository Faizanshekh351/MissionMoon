# Mission Moon (3D Planetary Ascent & Space Exploration)

Interactive 3D low-gravity vertical slice engineering suite showcasing dynamic surface traversal, cosmic physics, and cinematic mission storytelling powered by Three.js and AntSeed AI VPN telemetry.

---

## Quick Start Guide

### 1. Interactive 3D WebGL Game (Immediate Testing)
You can immediately play the game in your web browser:
- Double-click [`Launch_Game.bat`](file:///d:/Games/MissionMoon/Launch_Game.bat) or open [`MissionMoon.html`](file:///d:/Games/MissionMoon/MissionMoon.html) in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
- **Controls:**
  - `W`, `A`, `S`, `D`: Move along whichever surface the character is aligned with.
  - `Shift`: Sprint leap across hurdles.
  - `Space`: Jump / Double-jump with jet thrusters.
  - `1`, `2`, `3`: Align character surface normals (Floor, Wall, Ceiling).
  - `Mouse Drag`: Orbit celestial camera.
  - `9` / `L`: Instant Starship launch test shortcut.
  - `ESC` / `Space`: Skip intro or outro cinematic sequence.

## Gameplay Mechanics
1. **18 Cosmic Hurdles**: Climb from Earth's lush meadow through rotating monoliths, balance beams, and super trampolines to the final summit.
2. **Free Exploration on Final Planet**: Roam the Earth summit freely before boarding the Starship.
3. **Jump-To-Board Rocket Launch**: Jump onto the Starship to initiate escape velocity with 3D split-text GAME OVER and a 5-second cosmic flight.
4. **Cinematic Outro**: Three-part narrated epilogue showing Zaddr's journey to the Moon.
5. **Auto-Escalating Difficulty**: Progresses across Casual, Challenger, and Hardcore ranks.
